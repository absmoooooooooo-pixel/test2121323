Weight Calculation Formula

s = √(h² + (R − r)²),   A = π(R + r)s,   W = A × t × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · D = Large end diameter (mm) · d = Small end diameter (mm) · h = Height (mm) · t = Plate thickness (mm) · R=D/2, r=d/2 · s = Slant height · A = Lateral area (mm²) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Conical Reducer
  1. Convert diameters, height, and plate thickness to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate radii R = D/2 and r = d/2, and slant height s = √(h² + (R − r)²).
  3. Calculate lateral surface area of the frustum: A = π × (R + r) × s.
  4. Calculate plate volume V = A × t (mm³) and weight W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg), where ρ is in g/cm³.
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight of multiple reducers.

Variables: This calculator is for a hollow conical reducer (frustum) fabricated from plate. R and r are end radii, h is vertical height, t is plate thickness, s is slant height, A is lateral surface area. Density is in g/cm³.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a hollow conical frustum (reducer) made from plate of uniform thickness with ends open (lateral surface only). Does not include flanges, seams/weld allowances, or end plates unless modeled separately. Actual weight may vary by fabrication method and tolerances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a conical reducer?

Find the slant height s = √(h² + (R − r)²), then the lateral area A = π × (R + r) × s, and finally W = A × t × ρ. This gives the mass of the plate in the cone, not a solid cone.

What does a 200 mm to 100 mm reducer weigh at 150 mm high with a 5 mm wall?

The slant height is 158.1 mm and the lateral area about 74,513 mm², so the reducer weighs roughly 2.92 kg in mild steel.

What is slant height and why is the vertical height not used directly?

The plate surface runs along the sloping face, so its area depends on the slant height. The vertical height alone would underestimate the area, which is why the calculator converts it first.

How is this different from the solid cone calculator?

This page treats a hollow plate cone with a known wall thickness, like a fabricated reducer. The solid cone page uses the full cone volume, which would massively overstate the weight of a reducer.

How do I find the flat pattern size for a reducer?

The developed area A is the plate area you need; a rough blank uses the mean circumference π(R + r) times the slant height, with extra allowance for joints and trimming.

Are knuckle radii or straight flanges included?

No. The reducer is treated as a plain conical frustum of constant plate thickness, so formed knuckles, flanges and weld bevels are additional.

What if the two diameters are almost the same?

The shape approaches a cylinder. The formula still works, but the shell calculator is simpler and gives the same result for equal diameters.

Is the height entered the vertical or the slanted length?

Enter the vertical height between the two end planes. The calculator derives the slant length from the height and the radius difference.

How do I get the weight of ten reducers?

Enter one reducer's dimensions and set Quantity to 10; the total weight and metric tonnage cover the batch.
