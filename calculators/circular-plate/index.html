Weight Calculation Formula

W = (π ÷ 4) × D² × T × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · D = Diameter (mm) · T = Thickness (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Circular Plate
  1. Convert the diameter and thickness to millimetres (mm) if they were entered in inches (1 in = 25.4 mm).
  2. Calculate the circular face area: A = (π ÷ 4) × D² (mm²).
  3. Calculate the volume: V = A × T (mm³).
  4. Calculate the weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by the quantity of pieces to get the total weight.

Variables: D is the plate diameter, T is the plate thickness and ρ is the material density.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a flat, solid disc of uniform thickness with no holes, cut-outs, chamfers or edge bevels. Actual weight may vary with rolling tolerances and material grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a circular plate?

A round plate is a cylinder of thickness T. This calculator uses (π ÷ 4) × D² × T for the volume, multiplies by the density and divides by 10⁶ when dimensions are in millimetres, so you get kilograms directly.

What is the weight of a 500 mm diameter, 10 mm thick MS plate?

The area is 196,350 mm² and the volume 1,963,495 mm³, so the disc weighs about 15.41 kg in mild steel at 7.85 g/cm³.

How much does a 300 mm diameter disc weigh at 8 mm thick?

About 4.44 kg in mild steel. Halving the diameter to 300 mm and reducing the thickness to 8 mm removes most of the volume compared with a 500 × 10 mm disc.

What is the weight of a round plate per square metre?

Convert the disc to its area with π × D² ÷ 4, then use 7.85 kg per m² for every millimetre of mild-steel thickness. A 10 mm plate is 78.5 kg/m² and a 500 mm disc covers 0.196 m².

Can I use this calculator for a ring or flange with a hole in the centre?

Not directly. Calculate the full disc, calculate a disc of the hole diameter, and subtract the second weight. A ring weighs (π ÷ 4) × (D² − d²) × T × ρ ÷ 10⁶.

Why is a round plate lighter than a square plate of the same width?

A circle fills only π ÷ 4, about 78.5%, of the square that encloses it. A 500 mm disc therefore weighs roughly three quarters of a 500 × 500 mm plate of the same thickness.

Should I enter the outside diameter or the radius?

Always the diameter, in millimetres or inches depending on the unit button. The calculator halves it internally, so entering a radius would make the result four times too small.

Can I get the weight of a circular plate in inches?

Yes. Switch to Inches, enter the diameter and thickness in inches, and the result is still shown in kilograms. To convert by hand, multiply each inch value by 25.4 mm before using the formula.

Why does the cut disc weigh slightly less than the calculated value?

The result is theoretical: plate thickness is nominal, the sheared or laser-cut edge may be slightly bevelled, and the mill's density for the actual grade can differ a little from the preset value.
