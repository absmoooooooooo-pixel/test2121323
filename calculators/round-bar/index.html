Weight Calculation Formula

W = (π ÷ 4) × D² × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · D = Diameter (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³) · π ≈ 3.14159

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Round Bar
  1. Convert all dimensions to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches (1 in = 25.4 mm).
  2. Calculate the cross-sectional area of the solid circle: A = (π ÷ 4) × D² (mm²).
  3. Calculate volume: V = A × L (mm³).
  4. Multiply volume by density and divide by 1,000,000 to get weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 10⁶ (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: D is the outer diameter, L is the bar length, and ρ is material density. Result is theoretical weight for a solid round bar.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a straight, solid, uniform-diameter cylinder. Actual weight may vary with rolling tolerances, surface condition, and material grade/composition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a round bar?

The cross-section is a circle, so the area is (π ÷ 4) × D². Multiply by the length and the density to get the mass; in millimetres the whole expression is (π ÷ 4) × D² × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶.

What is the weight of a 20 mm round bar per metre?

About 2.47 kg/m in mild steel, because the area is 314.2 mm². This is the familiar shortcut D² ÷ 162 for steel bars in millimetres.

What does a 25 mm round bar weigh per metre and per 6 m length?

3.85 kg/m and roughly 23.1 kg for a 6 m bar. Mill bundles are ordered by weight, so the 6 m figure is the one to use for transport planning.

Where does the D² ÷ 162 rule come from?

It is the exact circle formula with the density folded in: (π ÷ 4) × 7,850 ÷ 1,000,000 is close to 1 ÷ 162. It is accurate enough for steel quoting and slightly wrong for other metals.

What is the weight of a 12 mm bar per metre?

About 0.89 kg/m in steel. The same calculation covers 8 mm (0.40 kg/m) and 16 mm (1.58 kg/m) bars, which is why the D² shortcut is used on site.

How much heavier is a square bar than a round bar of the same size?

A square of side D has 4 ÷ π, about 1.27 times the area of a circle of diameter D. A 25 mm square bar therefore weighs 4.91 kg/m against 3.85 kg/m for a 25 mm round bar.

Is the weight of TMT rebar the same as this round bar result?

The geometry is the same, so the nominal mass per metre matches the plain round bar of that diameter. Ribs and lugs add a small amount of steel that the nominal calculation does not include.

How do I weigh a bundle of 100 pieces, each 6 m long?

Enter 6000 mm as the length and 100 as the quantity, or multiply the per-metre weight by 600 m. The calculator's total weight and tonne figure cover the whole order.

What density applies to stainless or aluminium round bar?

Use 8.00 g/cm³ for stainless steel and 2.70 g/cm³ for aluminium, or type your grade's density. A 50 mm stainless bar is about 1.9% heavier than the mild-steel result.
