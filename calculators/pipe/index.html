Weight Calculation Formula

W = (π ÷ 4) × (OD² − ID²) × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶,   ID = OD − 2t

W = Weight (kg) · OD = Outside diameter (mm) · t = Wall thickness (mm) · ID = Inside diameter (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Pipe
  1. Convert OD, wall thickness, and length to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate inside diameter: ID = OD − 2t. Ensure wall thickness is less than half of OD.
  3. Calculate hollow cross-sectional area: A = (π/4) × (OD² − ID²) (mm²).
  4. Calculate weight: W = A × L × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: OD is outer diameter, t is wall thickness, ID is inner diameter, L is length, and ρ is material density. Valid geometry requires t < OD/2.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a straight, uniform circular hollow pipe with constant wall thickness. Actual weight may vary due to tolerances, ovality, seam/weld, and material grade.

Calculation Example

Carbon Steel Pipe
Outside Diameter (OD) 60 mm
Wall Thickness (t) 5 mm
Length (L) 1000 mm
Material Density (ρ) 7.85 g/cm³ Carbon Steel
Calculation

ID = 60 − 2×5 = 50 mm;   W = (π ÷ 4) × (60² − 50²) × 1000 × 7.85 ÷ 10⁶

× A = (π/4)×(3600 − 2500) ≈ 863.938 mm²; W ≈ 6.782 kg per metre.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a pipe per metre?

Use the annulus between the outside and inside diameters: (π ÷ 4) × (OD² − ID²) × length × density, with ID = OD − 2t. In millimetres this is the same as (OD − t) × t × 0.02466 kg/m for steel.

What is the weight of a 60 mm OD pipe with a 5 mm wall?

The inside diameter is 50 mm, the metal area is 863.9 mm², and the pipe weighs about 6.78 kg per metre in mild steel. A 6 m length is roughly 40.7 kg.

What does a 2 inch nominal bore pipe weigh per metre?

A 2 inch NB pipe has a 60.3 mm outside diameter. With a 3.9 mm wall it weighs about 5.43 kg/m; substitute the actual wall thickness of your schedule to get the exact figure.

Why is the inside diameter calculated as OD − 2t?

The wall thickness is present on both sides of the bore, so it is subtracted twice from the outside diameter. Using OD − t by mistake would overstate the metal area.

Which wall thickness should I use for a Schedule pipe?

Take the wall thickness from the schedule table for that nominal size and material, not from the nominal bore. The calculator needs the real wall thickness because the weight depends on it directly.

Does the result include threads, sockets or coatings?

No. It is the mass of a plain pipe of uniform wall thickness. Threads, couplings, flanges, cement lining and paint are additional.

Can I use this calculator for round tube or CHS?

Yes. Tube and circular hollow section are the same geometry as pipe; the words describe the product standard and tolerances, not the weight formula.

How much does a 6 m length of 60 × 5 mm pipe weigh?

About 40.7 kg in mild steel at 6.78 kg/m. Enter 6000 mm as the length and set Quantity if you need a bundle total.

How accurate is the pipe weight for seamless and welded pipe?

Both are calculated the same way from nominal dimensions. The main uncertainty is the mill's wall-thickness tolerance, since the mass is proportional to wall thickness.
