Weight Calculation Formula

W = [(π ÷ 4) × D² − (√3 ÷ 2) × A²] × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · D = Outer diameter (mm) · A = Hexagonal hole across-flats (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Round Bar with Hex Hole
  1. Convert D, A, and L to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate outer circular area: Acircle = (π/4) × D² (mm²).
  3. Calculate hexagonal hole area (across-flats A): Ahex = (√3/2) × A² (mm²).
  4. Compute net area = Acircle − Ahex, then W = net area × L × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: D is the outer round bar diameter, A is the across-flats size of the concentric regular hexagonal hole, L is length, and ρ is density. Hex hole must fit inside the circle.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a solid round bar with a concentric regular hexagonal through-hole. For a regular hexagon to fit, A must be within the circle limits (A ≤ (√3/2)×D). Actual weight may vary with tolerances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the weight of a round bar with a hexagonal hole calculated?

Subtract the hexagon from the circle: (π ÷ 4) × D² − (√3 ÷ 2) × A² gives the metal area, which is multiplied by the length and density.

What does a 50 mm round bar with a 20 mm hex hole weigh per metre?

The circle is 1,963.5 mm² and the hexagon removes 346.4 mm², leaving 1,617.1 mm², which is about 12.69 kg/m. The same bar without the hole weighs 15.41 kg/m.

What is the largest hex hole that fits in a round bar?

The across-flats size must not exceed 0.866 × D, because a regular hexagon inscribed in a circle of diameter D has across-flats 0.866D. Larger values would cut through the bar surface.

Where is hexagon-bored round bar used?

It is stock for parts that need a round outside and a hex socket inside, such as socket screws, hexagon drive couplings, valve stems and special fasteners.

Why is the hexagon area subtracted rather than the across-corners circle?

The broached hole is hexagonal, so the material actually removed is the hexagon area (√3 ÷ 2) × A², not the circle that would enclose it. Using the circle would overstate the material removed.

Does the calculation assume the hex hole is through the whole bar?

Yes, a constant through bore is assumed. A blind hex socket or a stepped bore leaves more steel and will weigh more than the result shown.

How much weight does a 10 mm hex hole remove from a 50 mm bar?

About 0.68 kg per metre, since the hexagon area is 86.6 mm². A 20 mm hex hole removes 2.72 kg/m and a 30 mm hole removes 6.12 kg/m.

Can I enter the diameter and hole in inches?

Yes, use the Inches unit button and give both dimensions plus the length in inches. The output stays in kilograms.

Why might the delivered bar be heavier than calculated?

A hot-rolled round bar is normally supplied slightly over the nominal diameter, and a broached hexagon can be a little under size. Those tolerances move the real weight by a few percent either way.
