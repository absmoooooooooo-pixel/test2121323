Weight Calculation Formula

W = ((A + B) ÷ 2) × H × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · A, B = Parallel sides (mm) · H = Perpendicular height (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Trapezoidal Bar
  1. Convert A, B, H, and L to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate cross-sectional area: Area = ((A + B) / 2) × H (mm²).
  3. Calculate volume: V = Area × L (mm³).
  4. Calculate weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: A and B are parallel sides, H is perpendicular height between them, L is length, and ρ is density.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a solid uniform trapezoidal bar with perpendicular height H. Actual weight may vary due to tolerances and material grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the weight of a trapezoidal bar calculated?

Use the mean of the two parallel sides: ((A + B) ÷ 2) × H gives the cross-section area, then multiply by the bar length and the material density for the mass.

What does an 80 mm / 50 mm trapezoidal bar weigh per metre at 25 mm high?

The area is ((80 + 50) ÷ 2) × 25 = 1,625 mm², which is about 12.76 kg/m in mild steel.

Does the formula still work if the sloping sides are not equal?

Yes. The area of any trapezoid depends only on the two parallel sides and the perpendicular distance between them, so a right-angled or skewed trapezoid is handled by the same average-width expression.

What is the difference between this page and the trapezium bar calculator?

The geometry is identical; the naming follows the top/bottom-width convention used for trapezoidal sections. Use whichever page matches the labels on your drawing, and do not add both results together.

How do I model a tapered flat bar?

Treat the wide end as B, the narrow end as A and the thickness as H. A bar tapering from 80 mm to 50 mm at 25 mm thick weighs 12.76 kg/m, which is a useful check against the blank plate weight.

Is H the slant length of the sloping face?

No. H must be the perpendicular height between the parallel sides. Entering the slant length makes the section taller than it really is and overstates the weight.

How do I get the total weight of several trapezoidal pieces?

Set Quantity to the number of pieces. The calculator multiplies the single-piece weight and shows the order total, including the metric-tonnage line.

Can this calculator approximate a dovetail slide?

It is a reasonable approximation for a dovetail when the angled faces are treated as a trapezoid, but the undercut geometry means the true area is slightly different. Use the average width for estimating only.

What units does the calculator expect?

Millimetres with the MM button or inches with the Inches button, for both widths, the height and the length. The result is always shown in kilograms.
