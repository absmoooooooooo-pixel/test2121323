About Us

Making Engineering Calculations Simple, Fast and Accessible

Welcome to Engineering Calculators — a practical online platform designed to make engineering and metal-related calculations easier, faster, and more convenient.

We understand that engineers, fabricators, technicians, students, and professionals often need quick and reliable calculations in their daily work. That's why we created this platform: to bring useful engineering calculators together in one place.

What We Offer

Our platform provides a collection of easy-to-use calculators for engineering, metal weight estimation, fabrication, and other technical calculations.

Whether you need to calculate the weight of a metal plate, pipe, round bar, square bar, or other materials, our tools are designed to help you get results quickly without complicated manual calculations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify everyday engineering calculations by providing accessible, user-friendly, and practical online tools.

We aim to save users time, reduce repetitive manual work, and make technical calculations easier for everyone.
