Weight Calculation Formula

W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000

W = Weight (kg) · V = Volume (mm³) · ρ = Density (g/cm³) · lbs = kg × 2.2046226218

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Step Formulas
  1. Solid round portions. The plain shank, socket screw head and double-ended stud middle use the cylinder volume V = π × d² × L ÷ 4.
  2. Threaded portions. A thread is not a full cylinder. The physical material area is approximated by the mean of the major and basic root circles: At = [A(d) + A(d − 1.226869 × P)] ÷ 2. The tensile stress area As = π ÷ 4 × (d − k × P)² (k = 0.9382 ISO, 0.9743 ASME) is reported as a mechanical reference only and is not used for volume.
  3. Hexagon heads and nuts. A regular hexagon across flats s has area Ahex = (√3 ÷ 2) × s². Multiply by head height or nut height, subtract the derived 30° chamfer on hex heads and nuts, then remove the averaged threaded bore from a nut.
  4. Other head forms. Socket head cap screws subtract the hex socket from a cylinder; countersunk screws use a 90° cone frustum; carriage bolts add a spherical dome and square neck.

Variables: d = Nominal diameter · P = Thread pitch · L = Length · s = Width across flats · k = Head height · e = Width across corners · m = Nut height · b = Thread length · ρ = Material density (g/cm³). All dimensions use the selected unit; internal calculation is performed in mm.

Assumptions/limitations: Standard dimensions are nominal maximum material values. Hex head and hex nut 30° chamfers are derived from the across-flats dimension. Washer faces, under-head radii, socket edge chamfers and thread run-out are not modelled; the nut bore uses the average of the major and minor thread areas; a nylon insert lock nut is metal for its full height less a 1.5 mm nylon collar. Weights are theoretical and exclude coating, manufacturing tolerance and plating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the threaded part not treated as a full cylinder?

A thread has crests and roots, so a solid cylinder overstates its metal area. The calculator uses a geometric approximation: the mean of the major-diameter circle and the basic root-diameter circle of the 60° thread. The tensile stress area is a strength-design value and is not used for mass.

Why can the calculated weight differ from a weighbridge?

Standard dimensions are nominal maximum material values. Real parts carry manufacturing tolerance, under-head radii, plating and coating. Hex head and nut chamfers are modelled; socket edge chamfers and thread run-out are not. The result is a theoretical estimate.

What is the difference between the metric and ASME thread factors?

Metric ISO 898-1 threads use k = 0.9382; Unified inch threads to ASME B1.1 use k = 0.9743. The factor converts pitch and nominal diameter into the reference tensile stress area. Physical volume uses the thread root geometry instead, so the two thread systems do not change the theoretical mass independently of their actual dimensions.

How is a nylon insert lock nut handled?

The nylon collar is not metal, so it is excluded. The nut is calculated as a chamfered hexagon prism less its averaged threaded bore, with a 1.5 mm collar deducted from its full height.

Why are both kilograms and pounds shown?

The calculation is made in kilograms from millimetre volumes and g/cm³ density. Pounds are a convenience conversion using 1 kg = 2.2046226218 lbs; no separate calculation is performed.

How does quantity affect the result?

Quantity multiplies the single-piece weight and volume to give the total. The result table and each saved history record keep the quantity so you can see exactly what was calculated.

What is the difference between full and half thread bolts?

A full-thread bolt uses the physical thread approximation for its whole length. A half-thread bolt uses the solid shank cylinder for the unthreaded portion and the thread approximation only over the entered thread length.

When should I use a custom density?

Select Custom Density when your material or coating is not in the reference table. Enter the density in g/cm³; the same value is used for all fastener parts in the calculation.
